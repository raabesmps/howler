A. Browser voice (speechSynthesis)

The baseline: what the first prototype used. Zero cost, zero upload, works offline.

Limit: this audio never enters Web Audio, so there is no distortion, reverb, bass boost or louder-than-100% volume. Only pitch, speed and the voices installed on the device.

B. Sender's recorded voice + effects

Record a voice (up to 8 s), then it passes through the effects chain above. Needs the microphone, which needs https.

Privacy: in the real product this means storing a person's voice on the server until the howler is opened. The recording stays in this browser during the lab.
